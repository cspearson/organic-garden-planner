No-Till & Organic Basics

Why No-Till?

Tilling destroys soil structure, kills beneficial fungi and microbes, and releases carbon. No-till keeps the underground ecosystem intact so plants get better water, nutrients, and disease resistance.

Core Practices We Promote

  • Keep soil covered — mulch or living cover crops year-round.
  • Disturb as little as possible — plant into mulch or use broadforks if needed.
  • Feed the soil, not the plant — compost, compost tea, organic matter.
  • Diversity — polycultures, companions, and beneficial insects.
  • Permanent beds & paths — never walk on growing areas.

Getting Started This Season

  1. Mark permanent beds (no walking on them).
  2. Sheet mulch or add 3–6" of organic mulch.
  3. Plant through the mulch or in small openings.
  4. Add compost on top each season — never dig it in.
